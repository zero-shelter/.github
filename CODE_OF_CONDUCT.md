# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, constructive, and inclusive community.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and changes in good faith.
- Give feedback about work, not people.
- Respect different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Protect private, confidential, and security-sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, spam, and the
publication of private or security-sensitive information are not permitted.

## Reporting

Do not report incidents in public. Use the private reporting channel specified
in the relevant repository's `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers will review reports and
may remove content, limit participation, or take other appropriate action.
